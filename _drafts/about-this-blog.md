This blog is where I will write up technical work in more detail than a project page allows. Expect posts on:

- **Robot learning:** imitation learning, vision-language-action models, and what makes a policy generalise beyond its demonstrations.
- **World models:** learned latent dynamics and planning with them, the topic of my MSc thesis at the AMR Lab, TU Delft.
- **Reinforcement learning:** implementation notes from writing PPO, SAC and DQN from scratch.
- **Model predictive control** and the interface between learned models and model-based controllers.
- **Research reflections:** evaluation protocols, negative results, and lessons from running experiments.

The first project write-up is [MimicVLA](/projects/mimicvla/), on fine-tuning SmolVLA from phone videos of my own hand.
