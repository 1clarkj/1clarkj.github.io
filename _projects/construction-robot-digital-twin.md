## Technical approach

- Designed the mechanical architecture and kinematics of a full-scale robotic system for automated 3D-printing construction.
- Mechanical design, component selection and structural validation in Siemens NX, applying the relevant DIN, ISO and EN standards.
- Electrical schematics, system layouts and a complete digital twin, integrating mechanical, electrical and control requirements into a validated design.
