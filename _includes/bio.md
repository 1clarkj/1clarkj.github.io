I am an MSc Robotics student at **TU Delft** and a student researcher at the **AMR Lab**, supervised by Sihao Sun and Javier Alonso-Mora. My thesis asks whether *embodiment-agnostic world models* can plan for *embodiment-specific robots*, using an aerial manipulator performing contact-rich tasks as the case study.

I work on robot learning: deep reinforcement learning, imitation learning and learned world models, with a focus on manipulation skills that transfer from simulation to real robots. I came to research from engineering. I studied Electromechanical Engineering at KU Leuven, where I graduated first of 360, and during and after that degree I built a real-time control testbed at Keysight Technologies and torque-vectoring controllers at Formula Electric Belgium.

I am looking for **research engineering** roles in robot learning, reinforcement learning and world models.
